# Production Shot List

Not in the brief. Added because a calendar a one-person team cannot physically shoot is not a
plan, and "can this actually be executed?" is the first question a founder will ask.

**Constraint assumed:** one person, a phone, no studio, no budget.

## Kit

Nothing here costs more than a few hundred rupees, and none of it is optional.

| Item | Why | Approx |
|---|---|---|
| **Phone tripod** | Macro is impossible handheld. The single highest-impact purchase. | ₹400 |
| **Cream linen / matte paper** | Consistent backdrop across all fifteen posts | ₹200 |
| **Black velvet scrap** | Makes silver read as expensive | ₹100 |
| **One warm LED lamp** | Controllable light after dark | ₹600 |
| **White A4 card** | Bounce/fill reflector | ₹10 |
| **Polishing cloth** | Fingerprints are visible at macro distance and ruin every shot | ₹50 |

## Shooting jewellery on a phone — the six things that actually matter

1. **Use macro mode or move back and crop in.** Phones cannot focus closer than ~10cm; getting
   too close produces a soft image that no edit recovers.
2. **One light source, at an angle.** Flat frontal light kills the sparkle, which is the only
   thing the metal is selling. Side-light at roughly 45°.
3. **Lock exposure and focus** before recording. Autofocus hunting mid-shot is the most common
   reason a product Reel looks amateur.
4. **Clean the piece between every shot.** Fingerprints and dust are invisible to the eye and
   obvious at macro distance.
5. **Shoot in 4K, deliver in 1080.** Downscaling sharpens, and it gives you crop room for both
   feed and Reel aspect ratios from one take.
6. **Overshoot by 3×.** A 12-second Reel needs about 40 seconds of usable footage. You will
   always want more than you planned.

## Editing

| Need | Tool | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Reels | CapCut | Free, beat-sync, good text presets |
| Carousels | Canva | Build one template, reuse for all seven |
| Colour consistency | One saved preset | Apply to every asset without exception |
| Scheduling | Meta Business Suite | Free; enforces the three-posts-ahead buffer |

**Build the carousel template once.** Same typeface, same margins, same slide-number position
on all seven carousels. Template consistency is what makes fifteen posts read as one brand
instead of fifteen experiments.

## Grid check before Day 1

Open the profile preview and look at the first nine posts as a block, not individually. Check:

- [ ] Alternating light and dark tiles — no three heavy tiles adjacent
- [ ] The same cream/warm tone recurring so it reads as one palette
- [ ] Not three product close-ups in a row
- [ ] Every cover legible as a thumbnail at actual phone size
- [ ] Reel covers have custom thumbnails, not auto-selected frames

The grid is the landing page. Someone who finds Post 07 will visit the profile before they
follow, and they will decide in about two seconds.
