# 02 — 15-Post Instagram Launch Calendar

**Window:** Monday 5 October – Sunday 18 October 2026 (14 days, 15 posts)
**Account status:** brand new, launching from zero
**Machine-readable version:** [`../data/content-calendar.csv`](../data/content-calendar.csv) — import straight into Sheets/Notion/Later

> **On the dual labelling.** Every post carries both a **Day number** and a **real date.** The
> day numbers make the structure portable — shift the whole thing to any fortnight and the
> sequence still works. The dates are kept because several decisions cannot be justified
> without them: the calendar ends 19 days before Dhanteras, the Navratri colour guide lands two
> days before Ghatasthapana, and the two Saturday posts sit on the weekend before Dussehra week.
> Strip the dates and the reasoning goes with them.

---

## Before Day 1 — the pre-launch checklist

Nothing below works on an unfinished profile. Complete this by **Sunday 4 October.**

- [ ] **Bio** — what it is, who it's for, the trust signal, the CTA. Four lines:
      `925 silver & 18k gold plated` / `Everyday pieces, ₹999–₹4,999` / `6-month anti-tarnish warranty` / `Free shipping & 15-day returns ↓`
- [ ] **Convert to a Professional (Business) account** — no Insights without it, and you cannot measure anything in `05`
- [ ] **Story Highlight covers created and named:** `Reviews` · `Unboxing` · `Care` · `Sizing` · `Shipping`
- [ ] **Link-in-bio live** and tested on mobile data, not just wifi
- [ ] **Meta Pixel installed and firing** — this is the single highest-value pre-launch task. Every visitor from 5 Oct becomes retargetable on Dhanteras. A pixel installed in November is worth a fraction of one installed in October.
- [ ] **DM auto-reply** set for "price", "size", "COD", "shipping"
- [ ] **3 posts pre-shot and in drafts** so a bad day never breaks the streak
- [ ] **Insert cards printed** for the UGC engine (see `05`)

## Week 1 (Days 1–7) — Introduce and earn trust · 5–11 Oct

> **Goal:** a stranger should be able to answer "what is this brand, and can I trust it?" by Sunday.
> No hard selling this week. You have not earned the ask yet.

### Post 01 · **Day 1** · Mon 5 Oct · 12:30 PM · Carousel (6 slides) · `BRAND` · TOFU
**Working title:** "We started because ₹1,899 shouldn't feel like a compromise."

The launch post. **Not** a product shot — a point of view. Slide 1 is the line above on plain
cream. Slides 2–5 tell the origin in one sentence each. Slide 6 is the hero pendant, small,
almost incidental.

*Why first:* a brand-new account has no reason to be followed yet. A product post on day one
asks for a purchase from someone who does not know you exist. A point of view asks for a follow,
which is the only thing you actually need on day one.

**Assets:** flat-lay on cream linen, one founder photo, hero pendant macro
**CTA:** "Follow along — we're building this in public."

---

### Post 02 · **Day 2** · Tue 6 Oct · 8:00 PM · Reel (12s) · `PROD` · TOFU
**Working title:** The hero pendant, one continuous macro shot.

No cuts. Slow rotation under a single warm lamp, catching the light. Trending audio, low.
One text overlay at 7s: `925 silver · ₹1,899 · yours on a Tuesday`.

*Why:* the self-purchase frame (`01 §2`) in six words. "On a Tuesday" does the whole job — it
gives her permission to buy without an occasion.

**Assets:** macro video, turntable or a hand slowly rotating it
**Caption:** full draft in [`03`](03-sample-captions.md#caption-1--post-02-product)

---

### Post 03 · **Day 3** · Wed 7 Oct · 7:30 PM · Reel (20s) · `TRUST` · MOFU
**Working title:** "How to check if your silver is actually silver."

Macro on the `925` hallmark. Then the magnet test — real silver is not magnetic. Then the cloth
test. Teach the viewer to test **any** silver jewellery, explicitly including pieces they
already own from other brands.

*Why:* objection #2 on the trust ladder (`01 §3`). Handing someone the test you could fail is
the strongest trust signal available, and it is the most saveable/shareable post of the fortnight
— saves are the metric that matters most here.

**Assets:** macro lens or phone macro mode, a fridge magnet, a polishing cloth
**CTA:** "Save this before you buy silver anywhere."

---

### Post 04 · **Day 4** · Thu 8 Oct · 12:00 PM · Carousel (5 slides) · `BTS` · MOFU
**Working title:** "Here's exactly where your ₹1,899 goes."

An honest breakdown: silver cost, plating, labour, packaging, shipping, platform fees, margin.
Real numbers. Slide 5 names the trade-off out loud — *"We don't have a showroom. That's the
whole difference."*

*Why:* objection #3. Unexplained cheapness reads as fraud; explained cheapness reads as
efficiency. This is also the most screenshot-and-send-to-a-friend post in the set.

> **Tier note:** cut this post if the brand is fine/bridal. Margin transparency reads as
> confident at ₹1,899 and as defensive at ₹80,000.

---

### Post 05 · **Day 5** · Fri 9 Oct · 1:00 PM · Static or 3-slide carousel · `FEST` · TOFU
**Working title:** "Nine nights, nine colours — what to wear with each."

The Navratri colour calendar with a jewellery pairing per day. Genuinely useful as a
save-and-return reference. Products present but secondary.

*Why the date:* Ghatasthapana is **Sunday 11 October.** This runs on the 9th because Buyer A
plans her nine outfits *before* night one, not during it (`01 §4`). Posting this on the 11th
would be two days too late for the person it is written for.

**Assets:** nine-swatch colour grid + one product per swatch
**CTA:** "Save this for the week."

---

### Post 06 · **Day 6** · Sat 10 Oct · 11:00 AM · Reel (18s) · `PROD` · MOFU
**Working title:** "One pendant, three places."

Three quick outfit changes on the same person, same pendant: office kurta → dinner dress →
garba chaniya choli. Hard cuts on the beat.

*Why:* cost-per-wear made visual, without ever saying the words (`01 §2`). Answers "when would
I even wear this?" — which is the real objection hiding behind "it's expensive".

---

### Post 07 · **Day 7** · Sun 11 Oct · 7:00 PM · Reel (15s) · `FEST` · TOFU
**Working title:** Navratri Day 1 — getting ready.

Get-ready-with-me, ending on the jewellery going on last. Trending garba audio. Posted early
evening as people are actually leaving for garba.

*Why:* the highest-reach slot of Week 1. Festival Day 1 is peak hashtag and audio traffic, and a
GRWM is the format most likely to be served to non-followers.

**Assets:** natural window light, mirror setup, one outfit
**Caption:** full draft in [`03`](03-sample-captions.md#caption-2--post-07-festive)

## Week 2 (Days 8–14) — Convert, and build the gifting case · 12–18 Oct

> **Goal:** first sales, first UGC, and a warm retargeting pool aimed at Dhanteras.
> The audience now knows you. This is when you are allowed to ask.

### Post 08 · **Day 8** · Mon 12 Oct · 12:30 PM · Carousel (5 slides) · `UGC` · BOFU
**Working title:** "Our first orders went out today."

Real photographs of the first packed orders, first customer messages (screenshotted with
permission, names blurred), the courier handover. Imperfect phone photos on purpose.

*Why:* objection #4 — proof that a real person receives a real thing. Week 1's audience has been
watching a brand with no customers; this is the moment it becomes real.

> **If there are no orders yet:** run the same post as "our first 20 pieces, packed and waiting"
> and shoot the packing line instead. Never fabricate a customer. One invented review is worth
> less than the entire account.

---

### Post 09 · **Day 9** · Tue 13 Oct · 8:00 PM · Reel (15s) · `PROD` · BOFU
**Working title:** The unboxing, ASMR.

No voice. Sleeve off, lid lift, tissue, the handwritten note, the piece. Close mic on the paper.

*Why:* this is the post that sells to Buyer B (`01 §6`). His third-biggest fear is that it will
look cheap when she opens it. Twelve seconds of good packaging kills that objection more
efficiently than any sentence could.

**Assets:** overhead tripod, quiet room, good mic or a phone close in

---

### Post 10 · **Day 10** · Wed 14 Oct · 7:30 PM · Carousel (6 slides) · `TRUST` · BOFU
**Working title:** "How to gift jewellery without knowing her size."

Buyer B's #1 blocker, answered directly. Slide 1: pendants, bracelets and studs need no size.
Slide 2: the string trick for rings. Slide 3: measure a ring she already owns. Slide 4: free
exchange policy. Slide 5: delivery cutoff dates for Dussehra and Dhanteras. Slide 6: the gift edit.

*Why:* the highest-intent post of the fortnight, and the one to put paid spend behind. It is
written for men, which almost nothing else on a jewellery account is.

---

### Post 11 · **Day 11** · Thu 15 Oct · 12:00 PM · Reel (20s) · `BTS` · MOFU
**Working title:** "Why yours won't turn your skin green."

The anti-tarnish coating actually being applied. Then the polishing bench. Then the QC check.
Voiceover, plain and unhurried.

*Why:* objection #1, the biggest single barrier to repeat purchase in demi-fine. Shown, not
claimed (`01 §3`). Also the post that earns the right to charge more than a ₹599 competitor.

---

### Post 12 · **Day 12** · Fri 16 Oct · 8:30 PM · Reel (12s) · `UGC` · TOFU
**Working title:** First customer reshare.

An actual customer's garba clip wearing the piece, reshared with credit and a thank-you overlay.
Their footage, their framing, unpolished.

*Why:* the first genuine UGC. It signals to every other buyer that posting your purchase is the
norm here — which is how the UGC engine in `05` starts compounding rather than needing to be
manually fed.

> **If no UGC exists yet:** reshape as a micro-influencer seed. See `05` — you should have
> gifted 10–15 creators in the week before launch precisely so this slot is never empty.

---

### Post 13 · **Day 13** · Sat 17 Oct · 11:00 AM · Carousel (7 slides) · `FEST` · BOFU
**Working title:** "The under-₹2,500 festive gift edit."

Six pieces, each on its own slide, each with price, who it suits, and the delivery-by date.
Slide 7 is the delivery cutoff calendar for Dussehra, Karwa Chauth and Dhanteras.

*Why:* Buyer B shops by price ceiling and deadline, not by product category. This is his post.
Pin it — it stays relevant through 8 November.

---

### Post 14 · **Day 13** · Sat 17 Oct · 8:00 PM · Reel (15s) · `UGC` · MOFU
**Working title:** "POV: he actually got the gift right."

Relationship-led POV skit on trending audio. Her reaction to the box, not the jewellery. He is
visibly relieved. Light, funny, ninety percent emotion.

*Why:* the broadest-reach format in the set, and the only post aimed squarely at the emotional
payoff rather than the product. Saturday evening is peak social browsing.
**Caption:** full draft in [`03`](03-sample-captions.md#caption-3--post-14-ugc-style)

*Two posts on Saturday 17th is intentional:* the weekend before Dussehra week is the highest
gifting-intent window inside this calendar. One conversion post at 11 AM, one reach post at 8 PM.

---

### Post 15 · **Day 14** · Sun 18 Oct · 6:00 PM · Carousel (5 slides) · `UGC` / `BRAND` · MOFU
**Working title:** "Two weeks old. Thank you."

The numbers, stated plainly — orders shipped, cities reached, reviews received, the one thing
that went wrong and how it was fixed. Slide 5 teases the Dhanteras collection.

*Why:* closes the loop opened by Post 01's "we're building this in public", and converts
two weeks of attention into anticipation for the 6 November peak. Naming the thing that went
wrong is the point — it is what makes the good numbers believable.

## What happens on 19 October

Out of scope for the brief, but the calendar is built as a run-up rather than a standalone
fortnight, so the handoff should be stated:

| Window | Focus |
|---|---|
| 19–20 Oct | Dussehra — auspicious-purchase day, one high-intent post |
| 21–28 Oct | Karwa Chauth run-up — Buyer B campaign, paid behind Posts 10 and 13 |
| 29 Oct | Karwa Chauth |
| 30 Oct – 5 Nov | Dhanteras run-up — the real revenue push, retargeting the pool built from 5 Oct |
| **6 Nov** | **Dhanteras — the single biggest jewellery-buying day of the year** |
| 8 Nov | Diwali |

Everything in the fourteen days above exists to make 6 November work.
